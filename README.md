## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer with **5+ years** of end-to-end experience building, deploying, and maintaining web applications and business platforms — from customer meetings and requirements gathering, through architecture and development, to infrastructure and long-term maintenance.

I mainly build with **Laravel, PHP, React, TypeScript, and Livewire**, with additional experience in **Python, Java, and Spring Boot**. I like owning the full lifecycle of a system, modernizing legacy applications, and finding ways to cut software, hardware, and infrastructure costs along the way.

- 🔭 Currently building ERP, route-optimization, HACCP/quality-control, and document-management systems at **ART MEDIA d.o.o.**
- ⚙️ Also handle full CI/CD and production infrastructure — Docker, deployments, monitoring, backups, and server administration
- 🌱 Constantly picking up new tools and technologies — always adapting fast to what a project needs
- 🤝 Happy to lend a hand in the dev community — always up for a good technical conversation
- 📍 Based in Križevci, Croatia

## 💼 Featured Projects

### 🚚 AI Route Planning & Optimization System
`Laravel` `React` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`
Route-planning platform that automatically optimizes delivery and service routes based on destinations, schedules, and operational constraints. Built the backend, frontend, optimization logic, APIs, and deployment infrastructure, then integrated it into existing business workflows.

### 🏢 Business ERP — Central Planning & Organization System
`Laravel` `React` `TypeScript` `MySQL` `Docker`
Custom ERP platform covering core business processes, replacing fragmented workflows and reducing reliance on expensive third-party software. A central system for tracking appointments, notifications, services, and clients.

### ✅ Quality Control & HACCP Management System
`Laravel` `React` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`
Digital quality-control and HACCP platform for managing inspections, records, procedures, and compliance workflows, with product identification at every step of the supply chain in line with food-safety objectives.

### 📄 Document Management Platform
`Laravel` `React` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`
Document-management system for organizing, processing, and controlling business documents, with role-based access, workflows, search, API integrations, and automated processing.

### 🎫 Ticketing & Support Management System
`Laravel` `React` `TypeScript` `MySQL` `Docker`
Modern ticketing platform for customer requests, issue reports, and internal tasks — ticket creation, assignment, prioritization, status workflows, and activity tracking, designed to integrate with existing business platforms.
